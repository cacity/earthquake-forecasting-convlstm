## USD 399 Pre-Submission Red-Flag Audit

Standard limits:

- One DOCX or PDF manuscript, up to 10,000 English words or 20,000 Chinese characters
- Up to 8 figures and tables
- Structural correspondence checks for up to 40 references; this is not full-text factual verification of every citation
- One key result table or up to 5 numerical claims selected by the authors

Deliverables:

- A Markdown or PDF audit report with evidence locations, risk level, recommended action, and explicitly unverified items
- A consistency matrix across abstract, main text, figures, tables, conclusions, units, sample sizes, time ranges, metric directions, and method names
- Checks of figure resolution, labels, captions, in-text references, and basic readability
- A 30-minute video or audio handoff; direct manuscript rewriting is not included

Payment: 100% before the audit begins. If the same manuscript upgrades to the USD 1,499 or USD 2,999 service within 7 calendar days, the USD 399 payment is credited in full.

## USD 1,499 Reproducibility Sprint

- Rerun or independently verify up to 3 main results

- Record commands, environment, inputs, outputs, random seeds, and limits to reproducibility
- Map `code → data → derived result → figure/table → manuscript claim`
- One consolidated re-check after the authors address the issue ledger

Payment: 50% deposit to start and 50% before delivery of the complete audit package.

## Research-Integrity Boundaries

The following work is not accepted:

- Fabricating, altering, selectively hiding, or inventing data, experiments, results, approvals, authorship contributions, conflicts of interest, or funding statements
- Selling authorship, impersonating an author, bypassing peer review, or guaranteeing publication
- Handling restricted data or confidential material without documented authorization and an agreed secure transfer method
- Rewriting language in a way that creates false substantive authorship or conceals unsupported scientific claims

Authors remain responsible for authorship, ethics approval, data rights, scientific conclusions, journal-system declarations, and the final submitted version.
